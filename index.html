Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Who we are

TripKit is a travel affiliate recommendation service. We help users find hotels, transport, activities and travel gear by connecting them with our partner advertisers.

2. What data we collect

We do not require registration or login. When you use TripKit, we may collect anonymous usage data such as destination searches and clicks, solely to improve our service. We do not store personally identifiable information.

3. Cookies

We use minimal cookies required for the site to function. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes on our own site. Our affiliate partners may set their own cookies when you visit their sites through our links.

4. Affiliate links

TripKit contains affiliate links to third-party services. When you click these links and make a purchase or booking, we may earn a commission from the advertiser. This comes at no additional cost to you. Our recommendations are based on relevance to your trip, not commission rates.

5. Third-party services

When you follow a link to a partner advertiser, you leave TripKit and are subject to that company's own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing their policies before booking.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or delete any personal data we hold about you. As we collect no personal data, there is typically nothing to access or delete. For any questions, contact us at privacy@tripkit.co.

7. Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
