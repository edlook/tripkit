Who we are

TripKit is a travel affiliate recommendation service operated from Germany. We help users find travel services by connecting them with partner advertisers from affiliate networks including Awin, Rakuten and others.

Data we collect

TripKit does not require registration or login. We collect no personally identifiable information. We may collect anonymised usage data solely to improve our service.

Cookies

We use only technically necessary cookies. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. Our affiliate partners may set their own cookies when you visit their sites through our links.

Affiliate links

TripKit contains affiliate links. When you click through and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Recommendations are based on relevance, not commission rates.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify or delete data we hold. As we collect no personal data, there is typically nothing to provide. Contact: privacy@tripkit.co
