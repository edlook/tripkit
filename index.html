Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Who we are

TripKit is a travel affiliate recommendation service operated from Germany. We connect users with partner advertisers from networks including Awin, Rakuten and others.

Data we collect

TripKit requires no registration. We collect no personally identifiable information. We may collect anonymised usage data (destination searches, clicks) solely to improve our service.

Cookies

We use only technically necessary cookies. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our affiliate partners may set their own cookies when you visit their sites.

Affiliate links

TripKit contains affiliate links. When you click and make a purchase we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Recommendations are based on relevance to your trip, not commission rates.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR you may access, rectify or delete any data we hold. As we collect no personal data there is typically nothing to provide. Contact: privacy@tripkit.co
